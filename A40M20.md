# Galaxy M20 on the A40 port's kernel

Branch `a40m20` = dipa-studio/ios-sweet main (patches 0001-0081) plus
tlfares/iOS16-on-A40's kernel patches (0082 = its 0000 Xcode iig fix,
0083 = its 0001 A40 port: XNU on the Exynos 7904's Cortex-A73s with 16 KiB
pages) and 0084, the Galaxy M20 touchscreen (Himax HX83112A on USI0
HS-I2C, bsd/dev/m20_touch.c), started when the ADT has /m20-touch.
Built with MACHINE_CONFIG=SWEET and libfirehose_kernel for -mcpu=cortex-a73.

The A40 prebuilt reached the iOS lock screen on the M20 on 2026-11-13 (see
M20LTE_PROGRESS.md on branch m20lte). Boot image: loader16 + payload from
mkpayload.py with the A40 ADT (dram-size 1 GiB for the 3 GB M20, the
a40-touch node renamed m20-touch) and cmdline + `ufsroot=USERDATA`.

## 0085: A73 clock (bsd/dev/m20_cpufreq.c)

Once at boot: ASV fuses must be 0x00090009/0x14 (speed group 9, table 20,
as ztOS's zrn-cpufreq.c), the A73 PLL at the 1352 MHz setting (M=156 P=3
S=0) and BUCK2 at 806.25 mV read back through ACPM (S2MPU08 on APM channel
2: SRAM 0x02052000 + 0x4b80 channel table, doorbell 0x11c40000, mainline
exynos-acpm message format). Then BUCK2 up to the level's voltage, the PLL
restarted parked on the oscillator (up to 5 tries), and a timed loop
compares the speed. Boot-arg m20a73mhz = 1768 (default), 1664, 1560 or 1352
(no change). There is no thermal throttling.

0085 build (run 43367520098) flashed (BOOT sha256 dd0850d9...): boots to the
lock screen, touch works, and the user reports it feels faster (2026-11-13).
The `M20 CPU:` log lines (checks, BUCK2 readback, loop ratio) are still to
be read from the USERDATA log ring.

## 0086: XNU RAM in several banks

wardevsa/ios-a5xelte (herolte) patches 0326-0329 (from HackiOS): extra RAM
banks from /chosen/hakios-ram-banks (boot-arg ram_banks=0 turns it off),
pmap object offsets by page index, VA congruent with PA. One hunk of 0326
(ARM64_PHYSMAP_ALIGNMENT_PADDING) ported by hand. tools/m20-adt-ram-banks.py
lists the M20's free pieces (see its header); first test --only 2 (+782 MiB).
