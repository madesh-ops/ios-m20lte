# Galaxy M20 on the A40 port's kernel

Branch `a40m20` = dipa-studio/ios-sweet main (patches 0001-0081) plus
tlfares/iOS16-on-A40's kernel patches (0082 = its 0000 Xcode iig fix,
0083 = its 0001 A40 port: XNU on the Exynos 7904's Cortex-A73s with 16 KiB
pages) and 0084, the Galaxy M20 touchscreen (Himax HX83112A on USI0
HS-I2C, bsd/dev/m20_touch.c), started when the ADT has /m20-touch.
Built with MACHINE_CONFIG=SWEET and libfirehose_kernel for -mcpu=cortex-a73.

The A40 prebuilt reached the iOS lock screen on the M20 on 2026-11-13 (see
M20LTE_PROGRESS.md on branch m20lte). Boot image: loader16 + payload from
mkpayload.py with the A40 ADT (dram-size 1 GiB for the 3 GB M20, the
a40-touch node renamed m20-touch) and cmdline + `ufsroot=USERDATA`.
